# ATTUNE revision record

Date: 18 September 2026

The revised proposal implements the literature-based scientific changes in the body of ATTUNE, rather than attaching recommendations to the unchanged text. It is a reconstructed, editable scientific working revision of the source PDF. The original PDF remains unchanged. It retains the MSCA institutional context and is not a completed FWO application or a claim of verified novelty across all literature.

## Main changes

1. **Title and central contribution, Section 1.1.** Replaced the broad anticipation headline with learning transferable personal cognitive-response models. The question is whether personal parameters represent reproducible dynamics rather than session/task fitting.
2. **Closest literature, Section 1.1.** Added explicit overlap with Chand, Xue and Vallée, plus earlier predictive operator-state and personalized physiological-control work. Removed the claim that existing methods do not predict how actions affect an individual.
3. **Wickens, Sections 1.1 and 1.2 Stage 1.** Distinguished the original four demand descriptors from Wickens's resource dimensions. Added a resource-overlap representation, matched pilot contrasts, and demand-only versus resource-aware comparisons. The benefit is a hypothesis, not an established finding.
4. **Hypotheses and objectives, Section 1.1.** Reorganized around reproducibility, representation-dependent transfer and sensing sufficiency. Added explicit failure interpretations rather than promising transfer in advance.
5. **Model scope, Section 1.2 Stage 3.** Replaced the initial 21-parameter workload/stress/fatigue model with a workload-focused starting model containing three candidate personal dynamic parameters. Sensor baselines remain separately counted; added latent-scale anchoring, recovery checks and partial pooling. Stress/fatigue are exploratory.
6. **Xue-related differentiation, Sections 1.1 and 1.2.** Shifted emphasis to empirical identification of latent cognitive dynamics and randomized human intervention tests. Clarified that the proposed primary tests freeze personal parameters; online state estimation is not online parameter adaptation.
7. **Chand-related differentiation, Section 1.2 Stages 2, 3 and 5.** Acknowledged prior richer-calibration/less-intrusive-operation patterns. Added separate-day frozen prediction, task-logs-only and no-teacher baselines, and explicit tests of EEG teaching's incremental value.
8. **Vallée-related causal discipline, Section 1.2 Stage 4.** Specified eligible randomized actions, assignment probabilities, outcome windows, carryover and refusal handling. Distinguished observed-outcome forecasting from intervention-effect estimation and unobservable individual counterfactual truth.
9. **Independent validation, Sections 1.2 Stages 3 and 4.** Teacher estimates are not ground truth. Forecasts cannot use future outcome measurements. Workload/performance outcomes and controller evaluation are kept independent of the controller's own score.
10. **Fair comparisons and decision criteria, Section 1.2 Stage 4.** Added simple personalized dynamics and action-conditioned recurrent comparators with equal information. Replaced “within expected variation” with a prespecified performance noninferiority margin. Added calibration, abstention and new-task uncertainty checks.
11. **Transfer, Section 1.2 Stage 5.** Separated transfer of dynamics, the observation model and task coding. Retained rich measurements for diagnostic evaluation while operational forecasts use eyes/logs only. Frozen and recalibrated results must be reported separately.
12. **Impact and work plan, Sections 2.3 and 3.1.** Updated outputs, gates, risks and effort priorities to match the new scientific claims. Retained the 24-month/24 person-month source envelope, while flagging its need for host-specific FWO replanning.
